# Species notes: review sheet

The notes shown when a visitor opens a field photograph full screen (species, Latin name, a short
description, size, IUCN status, range). They were researched on 2026-10-14 from Wikipedia, the IUCN
Red List (via GBIF), BirdLife, eBird and the Reptile Database. The site text lives in
`lib/speciesFacts.json`; this sheet keeps the sources and doubts.

**Please ask a naturalist to check the entries marked low or medium first**, especially where the
photo's name is general ("Green Frog", "Monitor Lizard") and the identification is only to genus.

103 photos, 103 with notes.

**Changed 2026-10-15 (photo audit):** 13 photos were removed from the site: 12 of species or habitats no trip visits
(Asiatic lion, greater one-horned rhinoceros, wild water buffalo, great Indian bustard, northern shoveler, two generic
waterbird photos, Avicennia mangrove, mangrove creek, palmyra palm, sea star, shore crab) and a duplicate Nilgiri tahr
photo, whose 'saddleback' note moved to `nilgiri-tahr`. Please check two rewritten notes: `nilgiri-tahr` (now shows a
male) and `tiger-cub-resting` (renamed "Young Tiger Resting": the animal is near-adult, not a cub). The files of
`asian-elephant` and `asian-elephant-tusker` were swapped, so the tusker note now sits on the photo with tusks.

**Changed 2026-10-15 (second photo audit):** `asian-openbill-stork` was removed: the site showed a head-only crop
enlarged to nearly twice its 394 × 852 px original, and no trip visits wetlands where it is a target. `osprey` was
re-cropped around the bird from the 6000 × 4000 px original, and `black-faced-langur` was trimmed to remove a car
window frame along its right edge; the identifications are unchanged.
