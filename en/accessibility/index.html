Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to plan a trip with us, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use.

Our commitment

Wildora Expeditions wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

This statement is about the website. For what our trips themselves involve, for example walking and access, see the FAQ or ask us.

What we have done

  • Pages are built with headings and landmarks (header, navigation, main content and footer).
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first thing you reach with the keyboard.
  • Menus, dropdowns, filters, photo galleries and the photo viewer work with a keyboard. Dropdowns close with Escape; in the photo viewer the arrow keys change the photo and Escape closes it.
  • The keyboard focus is clearly outlined.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off or reduced.
  • Text colours have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against the page and card backgrounds.
  • Form fields have visible labels. Required fields are marked, and errors are explained in text next to the field and announced to screen readers.
  • Photographs have text alternatives, usually the species or place name. Where the same name is already given next to a photo, the photo is marked as decorative so it is not read out twice.
  • The photo viewer has a button to show or hide captions.
  • Every map has a text description for screen readers, and route maps also list their stops.
  • You can zoom the page: zooming is not disabled.

Known limitations

  • The maps are pictures. Screen readers get a short description (and, on route maps, the list of stops), but not the visual detail, such as the shape of the route or the distances between places. Each trip's itinerary describes the route in text, day by day.
