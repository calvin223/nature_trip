What we collect

This website has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no analytics. We receive:

  • Your enquiry: what you choose to send us: your name, the country you live in, the trip and dates you are interested in, group size, children's ages, dietary needs, medical or mobility notes, your message, and the contact details you give us so we can reply.
  • WhatsApp conversations, if you choose to write to us on WhatsApp: your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and the messages and files you send. If you use WhatsApp from the enquiry form, the form only writes the message; nothing is sent until you send it from your own WhatsApp.
  • Booking and payment records, if you book: travellers' names, contact details and ages, what you have booked and paid, and [To be confirmed: any identity or passport details that lodges or park permits require].
  • Technical access logs, kept by the company that hosts this website when you visit it: for example your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser type.

Health information. Medical conditions, allergies, mobility notes, dietary needs that reveal a health condition, and the short health form we ask travellers to complete are health data, a special category of personal data. Please share them only so we can judge whether a trip suits you and plan for your safety. We use them for nothing else.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy (access);
  • have it corrected (rectification);
  • have it deleted (erasure);
  • have its use restricted (restriction);
  • receive it in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to someone else (portability);
  • object to our use of it on the basis of legitimate interests (objection);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it;
  • complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, for example the one in the EU country where you live or work or, in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise a right, write to the privacy contact above. We will reply within one month.

No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone, and we do not profile you.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. If you choose a language in the language switcher, your browser remembers that choice on your device; it is never sent to us. See cookies and local storage.

Children

When children travel with us, we receive their details, such as age, dietary needs and relevant health information, from their parent or guardian, and use them only to plan and run the trip.
