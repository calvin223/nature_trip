The right trip for the month you can travel

Each of our trips runs only in the months that suit its wildlife, its weather and the parks' rules. Here is when each one departs, and why.

  • The trip runs: fixed departures that month
  • No departures: off season, monsoon or park closure

Western Ghats (Kerala, Tamil Nadu & Karnataka)

Western Ghats Endemics Expedition

Our home ground

  1. JJan
  2. FFeb
  3. MMar
  4. AApr
  5. MMay
  6. JJun
  7. JJul
  8. AAug
  9. SSep

  11. NNov
  12. DDec

Departs on the third Saturday of October, November, December and January.

We do not run this trip in February and March: Eravikulam National Park (Day 4) closes each year for the Nilgiri tahr calving season, usually from 1 February to 31 March. October to December can bring showers from the north-east monsoon.

Departs on the first Sunday of October, November, December, January, February, March, April and May.

Tiger-reserve core zones close for the monsoon from 1 July to 30 September, and some reserves close from mid-June. We stop at the end of May, when daytime highs in central India can pass 40 °C.
