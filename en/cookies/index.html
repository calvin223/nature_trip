Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. Here is exactly what it does store, and what it loads.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, advertising, social-media plugins or other tracking tools, so no record of your visits is built up by us or for us.

Everything comes from this website

Everything a page needs is served from this website itself:

  • fonts: we host the font files ourselves, so your browser makes no requests to Google Fonts or any other font service;
  • photographs;
  • maps, which are drawn from map data bundled with the site, with no map tiles from outside services.

The company that hosts the website keeps technical access logs, as described in our privacy notice.

Local storage: your language choice

When the website is published in more than one language, a language switcher appears in the footer and the menu. Only if you choose a language there does your browser save that choice in its local storage, under the name wd-locale, so the site opens in your language next time.

  • It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • It has no expiry date.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

The website is currently published in English only, so the switcher is not shown and nothing is stored.

Links to other services

Some links lead to other services, such as WhatsApp, OpenStreetMap and the sources of our photographs and map data. Once you follow them, those services' own cookie and privacy policies apply.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or similar technologies that are not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
