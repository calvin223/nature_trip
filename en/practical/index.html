When you enquire

Choose a trip that suits your pace. On a private departure we can set the pace around your group.

Easy

Mostly vehicle-based safaris, walks in lodge grounds and gentle trails. Suits most fitness levels, including families.

  • Central India Big Cats & Forest Safari, 7 days / 6 nights

Moderate

More walking on uneven forest trails, some climbs, early starts and more exposure to rain, heat or cold. Needs basic fitness and comfort outdoors.

  • Monsoon Rainforest Nights & Endemics, 5 days / 4 nights
  • Himalaya Birds & Mountain Wildlife, 6 days / 5 nights
  • Western Ghats Endemics Expedition, 10 days / 9 nights

Challenging

Longer walks, higher altitude, steep trails, remote habitat or long field hours. Needs good fitness and stamina.

No current fixed departure is graded Challenging. A private route can be, on request.

Not sure whether a trip suits a health condition, an allergy or your mobility? Ask us before you book.

When you book

Three conditions of booking for every guest. Many of our routes are remote, and the nearest hospital can be hours away, so we plan the pace and our emergency response around what you tell us.

Travel medical insurance is essentialRequired at booking
Every guest must hold comprehensive travel medical insurance for the whole trip, covering medical treatment, hospital stays and emergency evacuation from remote areas. Check that it covers the activities on your trip and, where relevant, your age and any pre-existing conditions.
Tell us about any allergiesRequired at booking
Tell us at booking about any allergy, whether to food, insect stings or medicines. We pass food allergies on to every kitchen on the route. If you carry an adrenaline auto-injector or antihistamines, bring a spare and show your guide where you keep them.
Declare medical conditions before you bookRequired at booking
Tell us before you book about any medical condition, disability, pregnancy or mobility limitation, and any medicines you take regularly. We will ask you to fill in a short health form so we can judge whether a trip suits you, plan the pace and respond properly in an emergency.

Before you leave home

These you arrange and bring yourself. Each trip page lists what is included between its start and end points.

Getting to the start and home again

You arrange and pay for your own travel to the trip's start point and home from its end point, including international and domestic flights, trains and any hotel nights before or after the trip. The start and end points are under "At a glance" on each trip page; where they say "Confirmed at booking", we give you the details when you book.

Passport and visa

Make sure your passport is valid and that you hold the right Indian visa before you travel. Apply only through the Government of India's official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in: look-alike sites charge several times the real fee.

Your own kit

Cameras, binoculars, walking shoes, rain gear, chargers and a plug adapter are yours to bring; we carry shared field guides and references. The packing list on Practical Info covers the rest.

Personal medication

Bring enough of any medication you take for the whole trip plus a few days' spare, in its original packaging with a copy of the prescription, and keep it in your hand luggage. Our guides carry a basic first-aid kit but cannot supply prescription medicines.

Remote places, distant hospitals

Many of our routes are remote. The nearest hospital can be several hours away by road, and mobile signal can be patchy or absent. That is why insurance and an honest health declaration matter.
