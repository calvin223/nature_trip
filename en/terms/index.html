9. If we change or cancel your trip

Minor changes. We plan carefully, but local conditions change. We may make minor changes, for example to the order or timing of activities, and will tell you about them clearly in writing.

Park closures. Parks occasionally close or suspend safaris at short notice for weather, wildlife-management or legal reasons. If that happens on your dates, we substitute a comparable site or activity and tell you as early as we can.

Significant changes. If, before departure, we have to change an essential part of your trip significantly, cannot meet a special request we have accepted, or would need to raise the price by more than 8%, we will tell you without delay, clearly and in writing. You can then:

  • accept the change;
  • cancel and get a full refund, with no cancellation charge; or
  • accept a substitute trip, if we offer one, with an appropriate price reduction if it is of lower quality or cost.

We will tell you how long you have to decide and what happens if you do not reply: [To be confirmed: time allowed to reply, and the consequence of no reply].

10. Your responsibilities

  • Passport and visa. Make sure your passport is valid and that you hold the right Indian visa before you travel. Apply only through the Government of India's official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.
  • Health requirements. Follow the advice of your doctor or national travel-health service on vaccinations and other precautions, and meet any health requirements for entering India.
  • Travel insurance is essential. Every traveller must hold comprehensive travel medical insurance for the whole trip, covering medical treatment, hospital stays and emergency evacuation from remote areas. Check that it covers the activities on your trip and, where relevant, your age and any pre-existing conditions. Policy details we need from you: [To be confirmed: e.g. insurer, policy number and emergency number, and by when].
  • Accurate information. Tell us before you book about any medical condition, disability, pregnancy or mobility limitation, medicines you take regularly, allergies (food, insect stings or medicines) and dietary needs, and tell us if anything changes. We will ask you to fill in a short health form. We pass food allergies on to every kitchen on the route.
  • Medication. Bring enough of any medication you take for the whole trip plus a few days' spare. Our guides carry a basic first-aid kit but cannot supply prescription medicines.
  • Instructions and park rules. Follow your guide's instructions, especially on safety, and the rules of the parks and reserves we visit. If a traveller's behaviour puts themselves, others or wildlife at risk, or seriously disrupts the group: [To be confirmed: what we may do, e.g. exclude the traveller from an activity or the rest of the trip, and whether any refund is due].
  • Ethical wildlife watching. Follow the ethical wildlife-watching code on our species page: keep a respectful distance, never handle, bait or crowd wildlife, and do not disturb nesting or breeding animals.

12. Wildlife sightings

Wildlife sightings depend on the season, the weather and local conditions on the day of your visit. We plan routes and timings to give you the best chance, but no particular sighting can be guaranteed, so not seeing a particular animal is not a fault in your trip.

If a significant part of the trip cannot be provided as agreed, we will offer suitable alternative arrangements at no extra cost. If the problem substantially affects your trip and we do not put it right within a reasonable time, you may end the contract without a fee.

15. Unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances

These are situations beyond the control of whoever relies on them, whose consequences could not have been avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken. Examples are war or other serious security problems such as terrorism, serious risks to health such as the outbreak of a serious disease at the destination, and natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes or weather that makes it impossible to travel safely.

What they mean for cancellations is set out in sections 8 and 9. We are not liable to pay compensation for a problem caused by them, but we will still help you as described in section 13.
