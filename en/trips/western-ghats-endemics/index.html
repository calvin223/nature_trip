Western Ghats Endemics Expedition

A 10-day journey through the UNESCO-listed Western Ghats — rainforest bird sanctuaries, shola grasslands, and tiger country — focused on species found nowhere else on Earth.

  • 10 days / 9 nights
  • Moderate
  • Western Ghats (Kerala, Tamil Nadu & Karnataka)

What we look for

The habitats and species this route is planned around.

Highlights

  • Explore a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the world's eight hottest biodiversity hotspots, rich in plants and animals found nowhere else on Earth
  • Search for 20+ endemic bird possibilities including Malabar Grey Hornbill, Sri Lanka Frogmouth, Nilgiri Laughingthrush, and sholakilis
  • Look for endemic mammals: Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Tahr, Nilgiri Langur, and Malabar Giant Squirrel
  • Walk shola-grassland mosaics, montane forests, and lowland rainforest across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka
  • Finish in Bandipur Tiger Reserve with a chance of Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, Leopard, and Dhole

Species we search for

  • Lion-tailed Macaque
  • Nilgiri Tahr
  • Malabar Grey Hornbill
  • Sri Lanka Frogmouth

What you take home

  • Understand why the Western Ghats hold such extraordinary endemism
  • Identify key endemic birds, mammals, and plants in the field
  • Recognise shola-grassland, montane, and lowland rainforest habitats
  • Learn how the Southwest Monsoon shapes these ecosystems
  • Take home a personal species checklist from a world biodiversity hotspot

What you might see

Wildlife sightings depend on the season, the weather and local conditions on the day of your visit. We plan routes and timings to give you the best chance, but no particular sighting can be guaranteed.

Is this trip right for you?

Moderate

More walking on uneven forest trails, some climbs, early starts and more exposure to rain, heat or cold. Needs basic fitness and comfort outdoors.

Pace
Moderate, and on the move: five bases in ten days (Thattekad, Munnar, Chinnar, Valparai and Ooty), so you pack up and move on four times.
Terrain
Lowland forest at Thattekad, shola forest and grassland on the high ground at Pampadum Shola and Mukurthi, the Nilgiri tahr country of Eravikulam, and rain-shadow forest at Chinnar.
Days & weather
Cool to cold mornings on the high ground around Munnar and Ooty, and possible showers from the north-east monsoon between October and December.

Who will love it

  • Birders
  • Mammal watchers
  • Photographers
  • Nature lovers
  • Endemic species enthusiasts

Who it may not suit

Worth weighing up before you book:

  • Time on the road: the route crosses Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, with transfers between bases on hill roads. If you would rather unpack once, ask about a private trip with fewer bases.
  • Uneven ground on the high shola-grassland walks at Mukurthi, Avalanche and Emerald, which need steady footing and basic fitness.

The itinerary

Day 1 is the departure date.

  1. 1

    Day 1: Arrival at Thattekad Bird Sanctuary (Kerala)

    Visit the Salim Ali Bird Interpretation Centre, Thattekad Bird Sanctuary, and the Periyar River. Targets: Malabar Grey Hornbill, Sri Lanka Frogmouth, Malabar Trogon, White-bellied Treepie, and Rufous Babbler. Night at Thattekad.

  2. 2

    Day 2: Thattekad Bird Sanctuary

    A full day focused on the endemic birds of the Western Ghats in one of South India's richest lowland forests. Night at Thattekad.

  3. 3

    Day 3: Pampadum Shola National Park

    Explore Pampadum Shola National Park and the Vattavada region for Nilgiri Pipit, Nilgiri Wood Pigeon, endemic orchids, rhododendrons — and, if fortunate, the elusive Nilgiri Marten. Night at Munnar.

  4. 4

    Day 4: Eravikulam National Park

    Visit Rajamalai and the interpretation centre in the heartland of the Nilgiri Tahr. Also watch for Nilgiri Pipit, Black-and-orange Flycatcher, and Nilgiri Flycatcher. Night at Munnar.

  5. 5

    Day 5: Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary

    Cross into the rain-shadow forests of Chinnar for Grizzled Giant Squirrel, Indian Star Tortoise, Mugger Crocodile, and Indian Giant Flying Squirrel. Night at Chinnar.

  6. 6

    Day 6: Anamalai Tiger Reserve (Top Slip – Valparai)

    Travel through Top Slip, the Parambikulam border, and on to Valparai. Targets: Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Langur, Malabar Giant Squirrel, and Great Hornbill. Night at Valparai.

  7. 7

    Day 7: Valparai Rainforests

    Explore Nallamudi, Sholayar, and the tea-estate edges where rainforest fragments hold Lion-tailed Macaque and Nilgiri Langur, alongside Asian Elephant and Gaur. Night at Valparai.

  8. 8

    Day 8: Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve (Ooty)

    Visit the Government Botanical Garden, Doddabetta, and Wenlock Downs for Nilgiri Laughingthrush, Nilgiri Flycatcher, and classic shola vegetation. Night at Ooty.

  9. 9

    Day 9: Mukurthi National Park

    Walk the high shola-grassland country of Mukurthi, Avalanche, and Emerald for Nilgiri Tahr, Black-and-orange Flycatcher, and a rare chance of Nilgiri Marten. Night at Ooty.

  10. 10

    Day 10: Bandipur Tiger Reserve (Karnataka) & Departure

    A finale in Bandipur National Park with its interpretation centre. Chances of Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, Leopard, Dhole, Gaur, Sambar Deer, and Crested Hawk-Eagle before departure.

Getting to the start and home again
You arrange and pay for your own travel to the trip's start point and home from its end point, including international and domestic flights, trains and any hotel nights before or after the trip. The start and end points are under "At a glance" on each trip page; where they say "Confirmed at booking", we give you the details when you book.
Passport and visa
Make sure your passport is valid and that you hold the right Indian visa before you travel. Apply only through the Government of India's official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in: look-alike sites charge several times the real fee.
Your own kit
Cameras, binoculars, walking shoes, rain gear, chargers and a plug adapter are yours to bring; we carry shared field guides and references. The packing list on Practical Info covers the rest.
Personal medication
Bring enough of any medication you take for the whole trip plus a few days' spare, in its original packaging with a copy of the prescription, and keep it in your hand luggage. Our guides carry a basic first-aid kit but cannot supply prescription medicines.

Why these months

We do not run this trip in February and March: Eravikulam National Park (Day 4) closes each year for the Nilgiri tahr calving season, usually from 1 February to 31 March. October to December can bring showers from the north-east monsoon.
