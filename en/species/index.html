Why local specialities matter

Some species are widespread icons; others are tied to one region, habitat, altitude or season. In the Western Ghats, the lion-tailed macaque and the Nilgiri tahr live nowhere else on Earth.

Endemic and threatened species matter especially because they depend on particular habitats. When those habitats are damaged, there may be no second chance elsewhere.

On our trips we look for the endemic and locally special species that each route and season make likely, and we keep observation ethical and low-impact.

Ethical wildlife watching

We do

  • Keep a safe, respectful distance from wildlife
  • Photograph ethically, without pressuring animals for a shot
  • Teach identification by sight and by sound
  • Respect animal behaviour and habitats
  • Follow park rules on vehicles, routes and timings

We don't

  • Use bird-call playback to lure birds
  • Disturb nesting or breeding animals
  • Handle, bait or crowd wildlife
  • Collect specimens or eggs
  • Trample vegetation for photographs

Looking for something in particular?

Tell us the species you most hope to see. We will tell you which trip and season give the best chance.

Wildlife sightings depend on the season, the weather and local conditions on the day of your visit. We plan routes and timings to give you the best chance, but no particular sighting can be guaranteed.
