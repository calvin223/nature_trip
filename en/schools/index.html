A trip built around your group

Families, friends, clubs, companies, visitors from abroad, schools and universities: tell us who is coming and what you want to experience, and we will design a guided programme around your dates, pace, interests and comfort.

Every kind of group

Our default is a well-guided nature holiday, not a classroom field course.

Families

Gentle-paced nature days with safaris, short walks, birding, viewpoints, local food and enough downtime for mixed ages.

Friends & private groups

Your own departure on your dates, with a dedicated guide, a route shaped around your interests and a daily rhythm you choose.

Clubs & societies

Birding, photography and natural-history groups: target birds, mammals, butterflies or macro subjects, low-light rainforest sessions and patient field support.

Companies & retreats

Nature immersion, team days, lodge-based wildlife, landscape trails and guided forest walks, planned around your schedule.

International visitors

Route notes, comfort planning and guided wildlife time shaped around your flights.

What can be customised

We shape the programme around what your group wants: safaris, birds, mammals, forests, wetlands, mountains, night trails, local food, photography time and comfortable travel.

Target species
Tigers, leopards, elephants, hornbills, Western Ghats endemics, pheasants, frogs, reptiles, butterflies, orchids or other habitat specialities. We plan for the best chance, never a guarantee.
Trip style
Relaxed holiday, safari-focused, photography-focused, adventurous monsoon, family-friendly or academic.
Activities
Jeep safaris, birding, night walks where rules allow, wetland visits, waterfall stops, mountain trails, local markets and checklist sessions.
Comfort
Lodge category, safari intensity, walking difficulty, rooming preferences and food needs.
Length
Half-day guiding, weekend breaks, 4–7 day tours or longer multi-region routes.

Academic options when you need them

Our naturalists include past and present environmental-science lecturers at Bishop Heber College, Tiruchirappalli, with experience of nature camps and field-based learning for schools and colleges. Meet the naturalists

Short modules

Three to five day programmes introducing tropical ecology, species observation and conservation through time in the field.

Field courses

Longer programmes for colleges and universities, with field methods, data recording, student projects and review sessions.

Youth camps

Structured camps with age-appropriate nature walks, activities, observation skills and guided group reflection.

Safety & logistics

Every custom programme is planned around group size, ages, walking ability, weather, forest access, travel time, food needs and safe wildlife watching.

What we can arrange

  • Airport or rail pick-up, where practical
  • Transport between field sites
  • Accommodation matched to your comfort and budget
  • Dietary planning, with vegetarian-friendly meals
  • Park permits and access coordination
  • Route notes, packing guidance and target-species lists

Health & insurance

Required for every guest

Every member of the group, students and staff included, needs comprehensive travel medical insurance and must tell us about any medical condition or allergy before booking. Many routes are remote, and the nearest hospital can be hours away.
